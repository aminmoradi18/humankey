The phone tier

A paired phone may approve only clean mid-size payments: no risk flag, under $10,000, and paid to the same account as the vendor's last payment. Everything else goes to the hardware key.

  1. Pair with any six digits (the real server prints a one-time code).
  2. Open INV0001, the one payment in this run the phone may approve.
  3. Approve or Reject. The app checks the contract and signs with its own Ed25519 key, here in the browser.

This is the app's web build. Its queue and the gate's answer after you decide come from a real sample run: the real gate verified a phone's signature on that contract and paid (or recorded the rejection). Your browser's signature is not checked by anything here. The native app keeps its key in the phone's secure storage and asks for a fingerprint; this web build uses browser storage.
